# Katana/Cyberpunk Alley Scene Template

For KATANA-PROTOCOL and future samurai/katana-themed VØIDRIDE albums. Replaces the default fog-night car scene with a cyberpunk alley focus on the ronin figure and plasma katana.

## Scene Description

Ink-black rain-slicked alleyway at midnight, cybernetic ronin unsheathing a glowing plasma katana. Neon signs reflect in puddles on wet asphalt. Thick mist and fog. Dark cinematic 35mm film photography, anamorphic lens flare, high contrast noir atmosphere. Cyberpunk aesthetic with traditional Japanese samurai elements.

## Scene Prompt Template

```
[SCENE FRAME] in a cyberpunk alley at midnight, a cybernetic ronin [ACTION with katana],
[glow color] plasma katana casting [color] light on wet asphalt, neon signs reflecting
in rain puddles, thick swirling mist, cinematic 35mm film photography, anamorphic lens flare,
high contrast noir atmosphere, dark nightride aesthetic.

NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO CHARACTERS, NO WORDS, NO WRITING, NO NUMBERS, NO SYMBOLS,
NO TYPOGRAPHY, NO FONTS, NO WATERMARKS, NO LABELS, NO SIGNS, NO CAPTIONS.
```

## Album vs Track Covers

**Album cover**: Wide cinematic shot featuring the ronin as the central figure. Title centered vertically (no `--bottom` flag). Use the general scene prompt without per-track specifics.

**Track covers**: Per-track scene variations as mapped above. Title at bottom (`--bottom` flag). Each track's scene should match its mood (aggressive opener, dark second track, fast third, mechanical fourth, epic closer).

## Key Differences from Other Scene Types

- **No cars** — the ronin and katana are the hero elements, not vehicles
- **No moon** — replace with neon sky glow and distant lightning
- **Single character focus** — one ronin, one katana, one alley
- **Water and reflections** — rain-slicked ground is essential for neon reflections
- **Motion blur opportunity** — track 3 (iaido draw) benefits from frozen-motion water droplets
