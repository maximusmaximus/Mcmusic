# Particle Accelerator Cyberpunk Scene Template

Used for albums with a particle physics / containment breach visual concept
combined with a rain-slicked cyberpunk metropolis. Hybrid of the cyberpunk
city template and particle accelerator imagery.

## Albums Using This Template

- **POSITRON-BLEED** — first use, 9-track hyper-aggressive dark cyber phonk

## Key Differences from Cyberpunk City Template

- **Particle accelerator rings** on building rooftops replace standard neon signage
- **Subatomic particle trails** arc between towers like neon lightning
- **Ionized air glow** mixed with steam from vents
- **Containment breach/decay** vocabulary used in accent descriptions
- **Album color** is neon magenta (`#ff00ff`) — particle beam aesthetic
- Cars use **particle physics accent colors** (positron violet, Cherenkov blue, gluon cyan, lepton mint)
