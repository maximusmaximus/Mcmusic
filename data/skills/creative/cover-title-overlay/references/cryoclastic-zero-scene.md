# CRYOCLASTIC-ZERO — Antarctic Ice Cavern Scene Template

**Visual concept:** Crashed military transport frozen inside an Antarctic ice cavern lit by dying cyan flares.

**Used for:** CRYOCLASTIC-ZERO and future glacial/frozen/winter VØIDRIDE albums.

## Key Differences from Other Scene Types

- **Interior setting** (ice cavern) instead of outdoor highway or playa
- **Cyan/cold-white** color palette instead of amber or neon multi-color
- **Chemical flares** as light source instead of moon or neon underglow
- **Ice crystal fog** and **frost** instead of regular fog/mist
- **Corroded scaffolding** and **fractured concrete** instead of asphalt
- **Frozen vehicle** instead of running/revving car
- **Blowing snow** as atmosphere element

## Album Cover Composition

All 5 cars parked on cracked ice inside the cavern, each partially frozen at different angles.
Multiple dying cyan flares stuck in the ice at different distances, their flickering light
creating dramatic volumetric beams. Fedora/katana man standing in blowing snow and ice fog.
Title centered in ice cyan (#00ddff).
