# Cyberpunk City Scene Template

Used for albums with a rain-slicked cyberpunk metropolis visual concept.
Each track gets a different interceptor car, neon accent color, and atmospheric detail.
The car/color mapping below is a reusable default — the same 5-car palette works well
across different cyberpunk city albums. Vary the accent descriptions per album but keep
the car models (they provide good visual variety: muscle, JDM, Australian, American).

## Albums Using This Template

- **KINETIC OVERDRIVE** — first use, established the 5-car mapping
- **NOCTURNE OVERPASS** — reused the same cars with cyberpunk noir accents

## Key Differences from Default Fog/Night Template

- **Urban setting** replaces fog/forest — cyberpunk city with brutalist buildings, neon signage, manhole steam
- **Rain-slicked roads** instead of fog/mist — reflection pools mirroring neon signs
- **Interceptor cars** instead of vintage cars — modern/modded performance cars with underglow
- **No spaceship** — replaced by brutalist cityscape and neon signage
- **Lightning is colored** to match track accent (crimson, ice-blue, violet, orange, green)
- **Fedora man in coat** instead of kimono — urban noir aesthetic
- **Film stock**: Kodak Portra 800 pushed 2 stops (high ISO grain, dramatic night)
- **Extended negation list** includes NO GRAFFITI and NO WORDS ON PAVEMENT — city scenes are prone to text artifacts on roads

## Reuse Notes

When creating future cyberpunk city albums, modify this template by:
- Swapping car models per track (JDM imports, Euro sedans, American muscle)
- Changing accent colors per track
- Adjusting weather/atmosphere (rain, neon fog, steam, wet asphalt)
- Varying the time of night (deep night vs. twilight vs. neon dusk)
- Never reuse the exact same car across tracks in a single album
